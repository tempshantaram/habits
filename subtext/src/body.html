Working

Fix the words

A recogniser mishears words that sound like other words, and it has no idea what it is listening to. Tell Claude what the programme is and it can put them back — traders to traitors, a name spelled the way the credits spell it. Only the words are touched. Every in and out time stays exactly as it is, and you are shown each change before you keep it.

What is this?

Names, places, the words the programme keeps using. A sentence or two is plenty, and it is the difference between a guess and a correction.
