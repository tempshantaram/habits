Names & unusual words

Before a sentence containing a name, acronym or unusual word, it says the word, spells it, then says it again — once per passage. Those words carry a dotted underline.

Accent practice

Speaks English with the features of a Gulf Arabic accent, to train your ear against them. The words on screen stay in ordinary English, so you can see whatever you misheard. Rebuilding the sounds is only half an accent — the rhythm and stress are a device voice’s own, and those cannot be respelled.
