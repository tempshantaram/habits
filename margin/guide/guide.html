01Install it (5 minutes, once)

1 · HostPut margin.html in a folder, rename it index.html, and drag the folder onto Netlify Drop while signed in.
2 · OpenOpen the site link in Chrome on the Pixel.
3 · PinChrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. The icon opens straight to the capture box.
4 · SeedTap Set up now. Enter the expiry dates you have, then tell it when each dog treatment was last done.
Keep the same web address. Your data is tied to it. To update Margin later, drag the new folder onto that site's Deploys page, not onto a new Drop. A new address opens an empty app. Sites dropped without signing in are temporary and must be claimed within an hour.

02How alerts work

A web app can't reliably ping you when it's closed. So Margin passes anything time-critical to Google Calendar, and your phone sends the alert from there.

The test
Does the item have a clock time, a !, or an expiry date?

Yes → Hard ◆

A red → Calendar button appears. One tap creates the event, with repeats included where the rule allows. The row shows “in calendar” once sent, and “send to cal” again if you move it.

No → Soft

It stays in Margin, slotted into your day (commute, home, after bedtime). Once late, it moves to the top, turns bold after 3 days and gets a red underline after 7.
Pushed 3 times? Margin stops offering snooze and asks you to pick: Do it nowDelegateFirm dateSomedayDrop
In Google Calendar → Settings, set a default notification (e.g. 10 min before, and 09:00 the day before for all-day events). Expiry warnings arrive as all-day events.

04Where it fits in your day

Daily · from 20:00

Evening triage (2 min)

  • 1Empty the inbox: give each item an area and a when.
  • 2Overdue: move to a real day, park or drop.
  • 3Tomorrow: check the list is realistic. Add anything missing.
Weekly · Sun 09:30

Weekly review (10 min)

  • 1Stuck items: overdue, or pushed twice or more
  • 2Waiting on: chase or close
  • 3Next 7 days: flags any day with 6+ items
  • 4Expiry radar: next 90 days, plus undated items
  • 5Someday pile: keep, schedule, kill · 6 Back up

Review tab → Put both rituals in Google Calendar sets up two repeating events, so the reminder to open Margin comes from your calendar. Change the times in Settings.

07 · Your data

Everything is stored in Chrome on this phone, under the site's address. Nothing syncs. Every Sunday, your first tap saves a backup file to Downloads. A red banner appears after 14 days without one. Restore under Settings → Data. Clearing Chrome's site data, or moving to a new address, starts Margin from empty, so restore from the latest backup. Voice capture uses Chrome's speech service and needs a connection. Ramadan dates are estimates.

08Four ways things get in

Most of what you have to do arrives somewhere else first — an email, an invite, a message, a list someone sent you. The Sources tab is where those land. Margin reads them on the phone, suggests items, and waits for you.

1 · PasteForward or copy an email, then paste it into the box and tap Read it. Works with anything: a message, an invite, a list.
2 · GmailRead-only, straight from your inbox. Needs a one-time Google client ID and Margin served over https.
3 · FilesAn .ics invite, a .csv export, a .txt list. Open a file, or drag one in on a computer.
4 · ShareShare a page or some text to Margin from another app, or open the link ending in ?text=.
Nothing is added behind your back. Everything arrives as a suggestion with a line saying why. Add files it, Add & edit opens it, Skip drops it for good. The same message is never read in twice.

Each suggestion is filed into an area the same way typed captures are, and the title stays editable on the card before you add it.

11 · Where your mail goes

Nowhere. Pasted text is read on the phone and never leaves it. With Gmail connected, your browser asks Google for your messages directly — read-only, no sending, no deleting — and the sign-in token is kept in memory only, so it disappears when you close the app. Margin keeps a short snippet of the original with each item you accept; turn that off under Settings → Sources. The list of messages already seen is only used to stop the same email arriving twice, and Forget clears it.
